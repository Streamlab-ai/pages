Section 01
What LightSpeed VT Recommends
These aren't our recommendations — this is LightSpeed VT's own guidance on how to get the most out of the platform.
Use Assigned Training Templates
Build reusable templates by program type. One click assigns every course in the program to every learner in the cohort. No manual course selection each time.
Always Set a Due Date
Without a due date, no automatic reminder emails fire. The platform's notification system is entirely date-triggered. No date set = no reminders = learners go silent.
Use Watchdog for Monitoring
Watchdog sends automated progress snapshots to managers and client contacts. Stop checking dashboards manually — let the platform report to you.
Issue Certificates on Completion
Digital certificates fire automatically when a learner completes the program. Formal recognition drives completion rates and gives clients a tangible deliverable.
⚠️
The most common mistake: assigning training without setting a due date. Without it, none of the automated emails fire — enrollment, reminder, or overdue alert. The system is completely silent.

Pre-Enrollment
Before You Start
Confirm these four things before opening LightSpeed VT. If any are missing, stop and contact Manny or Tamien.
  • Cohort start date confirmed — in writing from Tamien or the client
  • Learner list confirmed — names and email addresses for everyone in this cohort
  • Cohort name agreed — e.g. "Think 3D — April 2026 Cohort"
  • Milestone Calculator open — Week 1 due date calculated and noted before opening LightSpeed VT
Enrollment Flow
The 10-Step Enrollment SOP
  1. 1
    Open the Milestone Calculator
    Open T3DU-Weekly-Milestone-Calculator. Enter the cohort start date and cohort name. Write down the Week 1 Due Date before opening LightSpeed VT.
  2. 2
    Log into LightSpeed VT Admin
    Log in with your admin credentials. If your login is not working, contact Manny or Tamien immediately — do not proceed until access is confirmed.
  3. 3
    Navigate to Assign Training
    Content menu → 309 Assign Training → click New Assignment.
  4. 4
    Select the T3DU Core Program Template
    For a standard 16-week cohort, always use T3DU Core Program. The template assigns every required course automatically.
  5. 5
    Confirm the Course List
    LightSpeed VT shows you the courses the template will assign. If anything looks wrong, stop and contact Manny or Tamien before clicking anything else.
  6. 6
    Critical Step — Never Skip
    Set the Due Date
    Enter the Week 1 Due Date from the Milestone Calculator. This triggers all automatic emails. Without a due date, the platform is completely silent. No emails fire. Nothing is automatic.
  7. 7
    Never: Sandbox or WhiteLabel Dev
    Select the Learner Environment
    Always select the LIVE T3DU learner environment. If unsure, contact Manny or Tamien before selecting.
  8. 8
    Add Learners via Content Role
    Add the cohort using the Content Role. Confirm the learner count before proceeding.
  9. 9
    Review Before Submitting
    Four checks: (1) Course list correct? (2) Environment is LIVE T3DU? (3) Due date is the Week 1 date from the calculator? (4) Email preview shows 3 notifications?
  10. 10
    Automatic from here
    Click Assign Training
    Click once. Enrollment confirmation emails send immediately. The 3-day reminder and overdue alert are now scheduled automatically. Enrollment is complete.
⚠️
If you make a mistake during enrollment, contact Manny or Tamien immediately. Before the cohort starts is fixable. After the first email goes out, it gets more complex. When in doubt, stop and message first.
Ongoing
During the Program
Week 1 — Confirm Launch
  • Confirm all learners received their enrollment confirmation email
  • Check LightSpeed VT that all learners show as enrolled
  • Flag any learners who didn't receive their email to Tamien
Weekly — Monitor Exceptions
  • Review Watchdog report for learners significantly behind
  • Flag anyone at serious risk of not completing to Tamien
  • Do not manually chase learners slightly behind — the platform handles that
At Each Due Date
  • Watchdog sends the overdue alert automatically
  • Review overdue list — note anyone critically behind
  • Escalate to Tamien if more than 20% of a cohort is overdue
Monthly — Client Report
  • Pull the monthly Watchdog snapshot for the client
  • Complete the Client POC Progress Report (see below)
  • Send to the client POC — do not send raw data
