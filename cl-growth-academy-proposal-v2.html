The Intelligence Layer — What makes this work

The brief isn't just a transcript pipe. Before any automation runs, we document and structure your marketing SOP into an AI-readable knowledge base — how you structure campaigns, what tasks each role owns, what a good brief looks like for an NDIS Google Ads client vs a Meta client. Claude draws on this every time it generates a brief, so the output is specific, role-accurate, and immediately actionable — not generic AI. This SOP training is the most valuable part of Phase 1A. Without it, the automation runs but the output is noise. With it, your IP is codified and the system produces briefs that your team can act on without a single clarifying question.

What gets built

  • Chris's marketing SOP documented and structured into an AI-readable knowledge base — campaigns, roles, task types, NDIS-specific context
  • Per-client knowledge profiles in Notion — ICP, brand voice, campaign history, compliance notes (replaces Gemini Gems)
  • Granola.ai connected to Zoom and Google Meet — auto-records every call from the moment it ends
  • N8N/Make.com workflow: call ends → transcript → Claude reads SOP + client profile → brief generated immediately
  • Notion set up as brief hub — every draft stored, searchable, version-tracked per client
  • Slack approval gate — Notify Chris with link, interactive Approve / Request Changes buttons
  • Asana tasks auto-created on approval — role-specific, drawn from the brief and SOP — team notified instantly
  • End-to-end tested with 2 real client calls before sign-off

1B

Full Chris OS

Month 2–3 Expand ▼

Everything else Chris personally does to run his agency — automated. From a lead appearing in your inbox to a fully onboarded client with a scope brief ready for the team.

  • Notion as lightweight CRM — lead pipeline, records, full client profiles+ more
    Every lead gets a Notion record with source, contact info, conversation history, status, and next action. Every client gets a full profile: brand voice, campaign history, transcripts, briefs, contracts, comms — one page, fully AI-readable. No Pipedrive. No HubSpot. No fragmented Gems.
  • Lead intake automation — web form + email inquiry → Notion record auto-created+ more
    A new inquiry arrives via your website contact form or email. N8N/Make.com detects it, creates a lead record in Notion, and pings Chris in Slack. Nothing falls through the cracks. No manual data entry.
  • Lead qualification scoring — AI checks NDIS ICP before a discovery call task is created+ more
    Before a discovery call gets booked, Claude checks the inquiry against your ideal client profile — NDIS provider type, service alignment, budget signals. If qualified, a discovery task is created. If not, Chris gets a Slack message with the reasoning. You only spend time on the right leads.
  • AI-drafted initial reply → Slack approval → sends automatically+ more
    Claude drafts the first reply to every new inquiry — in your voice, referencing what they said. Chris reviews and approves in Slack with one tap. Approved replies send automatically. You respond faster without writing from scratch.
  • Discovery call → transcript → follow-up email + proposal draft generated for review+ more
    Granola records the discovery call. N8N/Make.com triggers on call end. Claude generates a personalised follow-up email and a proposal draft from the transcript and lead context. Chris reviews both in Notion, approves, and they send. No post-call admin.
  • Contract generation + e-signature → Notion status updated on signing+ more
    Once Chris approves a proposal, a contract is generated and sent for e-signature. When the client signs, Notion updates the lead status to Client automatically. No manual status changes. No chasing signatures.
  • Onboarding questionnaire auto-sent → scope brief generated → client workspace + Asana project created+ more
    On contract signing, the onboarding questionnaire goes to the client automatically. When filled, Claude generates the initial scope brief. A client workspace is created in Notion. An Asana project is created from template. The team can start within hours of contract signing.
  • Invoice auto-generated at each milestone → payment tracked → overdue follow-up triggered+ more
    Invoices generate at contract sign, phase delivery, and monthly retainer dates. Payment status tracked in Notion — Sent / Paid / Overdue. If overdue, a chase email is drafted and triggered automatically after X days. You never manually track payments again.
  • Contract renewal reminder — Slack alert 60 days before expiry+ more
    A scheduled N8N/Make.com workflow checks all contract end dates in Notion each week. 60 days before any contract expires, Chris gets a Slack message with the client name, end date, and a draft renewal email ready to approve. No renewals slip silently.
2A

Agency Delivery

Month 3–4 Expand ▼

Where Phase 1 ends (approved brief), Phase 2A begins. Your team executes consistently, at quality, without you in the middle.

  • Role-specific Asana tasks — Google Ads, Social/Meta, and Designer each get a tailored brief+ more
    When Chris approves a brief, three separate Claude outputs generate automatically — one per role. Google Ads gets: campaign objective, keyword priorities, budget allocation, what changed from last month. Social/Meta gets: campaign theme, copy angles, audience specs, NDIS compliance notes. Designer gets: full asset list by platform, exact dimensions, visual direction, copy to incorporate. Tasks auto-populate in Asana with due dates. Team opens Asana, knows exactly what to do. No Slack messages from Chris required.
  • Internal QA gate — team completes work → QA review → then to client+ more
    Every deliverable goes through a structured QA task in Asana before anything is shared with the client. Reviews check against the approved brief, NDIS compliance requirements, and brand guidelines. Nothing leaves the agency unchecked. QA tasks are auto-created per deliverable when team work is marked complete.
  • Client asset approval workflow — Chris reviews, client approves, team publishes+ more
    Team delivers → Chris does an internal review in Notion → approved assets are shared with the client → client marks approved → team publishes. The full approval loop is tracked per client per campaign cycle in Notion. No approvals lost in email chains.
  • NDIS Social Ads Playbook — audience types, Meta vs LinkedIn strategy, compliance requirements+ more
    A structured playbook built into Claude covering: the 4 NDIS audience types (participants, support coordinators, plan managers, LACs), Meta vs LinkedIn creative strategy for disability services, proven copy frameworks, and NDIS compliance requirements that affect ad copy. This is what makes every brief NDIS-accurate, not generic AI output.
  • NDIS Google Ads Playbook — keyword strategy, campaign structure, negative keyword library+ more
    Google Ads-specific playbook: keyword categories and priorities for disability services, campaign structure by NDIS provider type, a negative keyword library to exclude irrelevant searches, and bidding approach by budget tier. Built into the brief generation layer so every Google Ads task is grounded in what actually works for NDIS providers.
  • Channel-specific brief generator — one approved brief produces three role outputs automatically+ more
    The approved monthly strategy brief triggers three separate Claude generations. Each output is tailored to the role's format, detail level, and terminology — and pre-loaded with that client's campaign history and brand context. One approval from Chris triggers all three. Team gets role-specific instructions, not a generic document to interpret.
  • Weekly performance check-in — Monday Asana trigger per active client+ more
    Every Monday morning, N8N/Make.com creates an optimisation review task in Asana for each active client. The task includes last week's performance data and prompts the relevant team member to flag any adjustments needed. Mid-month optimisation happens consistently — not just reactively at month end.
  • Client comms cadence — Friday AI-drafted status email, Chris approves in Slack, sends automatically+ more
    Every Friday, N8N/Make.com pulls that week's completed Asana tasks per client. Claude drafts a short status email in your voice — what was done, what's coming, any questions for the client. Chris approves in Slack with one tap. Goes automatically. Clients stay informed between monthly calls without Chris writing a word.
  • Google Drive campaign folder auto-created each cycle, linked directly in team tasks+ more
    At the start of each campaign cycle, a Google Drive folder is created per client with the correct naming structure. The folder link is embedded directly in the relevant Asana tasks. Team members never need to ask where to put work or where to find brief assets.

Reporting & Retention

Month 5 Expand ▼

The full system closed out. Client reporting automated, retention built in, team fully documented.

  • Reporting data pipeline — AgencyAnalytics pulls live Meta + Google Ads data automatically+ more
    AgencyAnalytics connected to each client's Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads account. Performance data — spend, reach, clicks, conversions, ROAS — pulled automatically each month and fed into a structured Notion report template per client. No manual data entry. The report builds itself from the data.
  • Monthly client report auto-generated — planned, executed, results, what's next, what's needed+ more
    Claude generates a formatted monthly report per client using AgencyAnalytics data and the approved brief from that month. Structure: what we planned → what we executed → what the numbers show → what's changing next month → what we need from you. Business-owner readable. Chris approves before it goes. Every client, every month, consistent format.
  • Chris approval gate — every report reviewed in Notion before it reaches the client+ more
    The draft report lands in Notion. Chris gets a Slack notification with a link and an Approve / Request Changes button. Only after approval does the report go to the client. Automated doesn't mean unsupervised — full oversight maintained at every gate.
  • QBR workflow — Claude generates a strategic quarterly review from 3 months of data+ more
    Every quarter, N8N/Make.com triggers a QBR generation for each active client. Claude pulls 3 months of briefs, reports, and execution data from Notion and generates a strategic review: what was done, what performed, what the recommendation is for next quarter. Chris reviews, adds his take, schedules a call. Consistent QBRs are the single biggest retention tool an agency has.
  • Team SOPs per role — any new hire up to speed on any client within an hour+ more
    Role-specific guides written for each team member: how to use the system, where briefs live, how to flag issues, how to handle edge cases. A new Google Ads manager joining mid-month can open the SOP, read the client's Notion profile, and have full context before touching a campaign. Reduces the time Chris spends re-explaining things to every new person.
  • Full system documentation — developer-handoff ready, AI-tool-agnostic+ more
    Every workflow documented: trigger conditions, node logic, error handling, data flows. If Claude gets replaced, if N8N/Make.com is swapped, if this needs to be handed to another developer — the documentation covers it. The system doesn't depend on any single person knowing how it works.
