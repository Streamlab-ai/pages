01 / 08 MattKrall.com — Website V2

Identity Audit-first entry, plus AI search visibility upgrades

A walkthrough of the changes from Matt's website revision handoff, fully built into the mockup — and the additional fixes we're recommending from an AI-search audit of the current live site.

02 / 08 The Core Change

The entry point moved from a conversation to an exercise.

Before

Request a Clarity Conversation

First ask was a 30-minute conversation before the visitor had experienced any value. Higher-friction, higher drop-off.

After

Start with the Identity Audit

First ask is a 15-minute self-guided audit, delivered by email. The Clarity Conversation becomes the optional second step.

03 / 08 New Visitor Journey

Recognition → Form → Identity Audit → Clarity Conversation → Next step

1
Recognition — visitor sees themselves in the problem copy.
2
Light form — name + email only; phone, role, and company are optional or removed.
3
Identity Audit — 15-minute guided self-audit, completed independently.
4
Clarity Conversation — optional, complimentary 30 minutes to make it specific.
5
Right next step — six-session 1:1 coaching, another form of support, or nothing further. All valid.
04 / 08 What Else Changed

Three new sections, one relocation.

  • "How I Coach" — new section under About, answers what it's actually like to work with Matt.
  • Six-session engagement, spelled out — cadence, format, what happens at the end. Replaces vague "Inner Game of Leadership" framing.
  • Outcome section — closes on "good coaching should make the coach less necessary."
  • Testimonials redistributed — moved from one block to sitting directly under the claims they support.
05 / 08 Decision Needed From Matt

Executive-only, or broaden the qualifying language?

The current copy repeatedly limits the offer to senior executives/operators by title. The Audit and coaching product itself don't require that title — just real stakes and willingness to look inward.

Development can ship everything else without this call. But hero language and some 1:1 section wording should be locked only after Matt confirms the audience scope.
