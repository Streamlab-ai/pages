09 Brand Document

What This Brand Is

Woolworth Lofts is a boutique loft-style hotel property at 953 5th Avenue in San Diego's Gaslamp Quarter — one of Evan's priority DTC properties. The building was constructed in 1886 as the actual F.W. Woolworth Company store — a Victorian-era commercial landmark that is now part of the preserved Gaslamp Quarter Historic District. The brand carries that original heritage into the hospitality space: one of San Diego's oldest standing commercial buildings, now reimagined as an intimate, character-rich loft stay.

Priority 1 property in the NOVIQ DTC rollout.

Positioning Statement

A Victorian-era landmark at the heart of the Gaslamp Quarter — where 140 years of San Diego history meet modern comfort, and staying feels like owning a piece of the city.

Ideal Guest Profile

PRIMARY GUEST

Design-conscious traveler, 30–55, urban professional or creative. Values authenticity and story. Pays a premium to avoid generic hotel chains. Discovers the property through Google search after seeing it on Airbnb/Booking.com. Would book direct if they knew the property had its own site.

SECONDARY GUEST

Weekend getaway couple or solo business traveler who wants a Gaslamp location with more character than the Marriott. Responsive to "book direct and get more" messaging.

Brand Origin & Story

953 5th Avenue. Built in 1886. Victorian commercial architecture. Originally the F.W. Woolworth Company store — one of the earliest outposts of the five-and-dime empire that would become an icon of American retail culture. The building stood through San Diego's boom years, the Prohibition era, two World Wars, and the Gaslamp Quarter's full arc from gold rush commerce to urban decline to historic preservation. Renovated in 1922 and now part of the officially protected Gaslamp Quarter Historic District, it is one of the oldest standing commercial structures in downtown San Diego.

F.W. Woolworth's stores were the original "accessible luxury" — places where anyone could walk in off the street and afford something good. That democratic spirit, translated into hospitality, becomes the Woolworth Lofts brand idea: a building with the pedigree of a landmark and the warmth of a neighborhood stay. Not pretentious. Not a chain. A real address with a real story, at the center of one of the most walkable blocks in San Diego.

→ Character, bones, detail

WE NEVER SAY

× World-class, state-of-the-art

× Boutique (say what makes it boutique)

× Cozy (too small-feeling for a loft brand)

Visual Voice

Dark backgrounds. Brass and warm cream. Wide serif typography. Never centered text for headlines — left-aligned, architectural. Minimal copy on images. Let the photography breathe.
