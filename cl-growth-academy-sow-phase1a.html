02 — Overview

Project Overview

CL Growth Academy engages StreamLab Ai to design and build the Brief Engine — an automated intake and delivery system that captures client call data via Granola, generates a structured client brief using AI, creates and assigns tasks in Asana, and triggers client-facing communications automatically.

This Statement of Work covers Phase 1A only. Subsequent phases (1B, 2A, 2B) are subject to separate written agreements.

03 — Scope of Work

Deliverables

The following six deliverables are included in Phase 1A.

01

Granola Integration

Connect Chris's existing paid Granola account into the automation layer. Call recordings and transcripts are routed automatically — no manual export required.

02

SOP + Knowledge Base Training

CL Growth Academy's marketing SOP is loaded into Claude as a structured knowledge base. This trains the AI on Chris's actual methodology before any brief is generated.

03

AI Brief Generator

A Claude-powered workflow trained on CL Growth Academy's marketing SOP. Takes Granola call output and produces a structured client brief — objectives, deliverables, tone, and timeline — ready for internal use and client delivery.

04

Asana Project Automation

On brief generation, a new Asana project is created automatically with tasks assigned, due dates set, and the brief attached. No manual project setup required.

05

n8n Workflow Architecture

Core automation infrastructure built in n8n connecting Granola → Claude AI → Asana → communication layer. Fully documented and handed over at phase completion.

06

Sign-Off Testing

Phase 1A is complete when the end-to-end workflow runs successfully on 2 real client calls with accurate brief output confirmed by Chris Lapa.
