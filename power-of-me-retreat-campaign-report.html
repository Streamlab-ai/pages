Key Takeaways

What Worked

Open and click rates crushed targets across all three segments — 5–10x better than the parallel cold outbound sequence. Zero unsubscribes confirms the community trusts Tamien's messaging. Mentorship Graduates were the standout: highest open, click, and reply rate despite being the smallest list.

Where It Broke Down

The gap was between click and purchase — not between send and click. Email got attention. The retreat landing page or booking step didn't close it. This is not an email problem — it's a conversion funnel problem to solve before the next event.

Recommendations for Upcoming Events

  • 1 Re-use these lists only for local events. Most contacts are Sioux Falls–based. These sequences should be re-activated when Tamien is running an event in or near Sioux Falls. For Kansas City, Jacksonville, and Denver, we build fresh local lists via Apollo prospecting.
  • 2 Prioritise Mentorship Graduates every time. This is Tamien's highest-trust, highest-engagement segment. Whenever there's an event in their area, they get first contact — and a personalised message, not a broadcast.
  • 3 Fix the post-click experience before the next event. Email performance was strong — the drop-off is at the landing page or registration flow. This needs to be reviewed and tightened before we drive traffic to it again.
  • 4 Run email and social together. The content calendar (in progress) will reinforce email messaging for each event. Contacts who see Tamien on socials and receive an email are significantly more likely to convert. Both channels go live together going forward.
