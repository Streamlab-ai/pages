Initial term

Attribution

Payment trigger
Revenue share is calculated only on cash collected, excluding taxes, refunds, pass-through costs, and unpaid invoices.
Coverage
The 5% applies to the first 12 months of collected revenue from the sourced client relationship.
Existing pipeline
No revenue share on opportunities already active in the CRM before the start date unless both parties explicitly classify them as reactivated by the new campaign.
Transparency
Think3D and Elevate You provide closed-won values and collections status for attributable opportunities.
Excluded from the base retainer: advertising spend, software subscriptions, direct mail/gifting, event venue and production costs, major core-company website development, third-party contractors, and travel. These require separate approval.

Why not commission-only?

The engagement requires fixed weekly delivery across strategy, CRM, content, campaigns, systems, and reporting. A stable base funds that operating capacity, while the revenue share adds meaningful upside when attributable work produces collected revenue.

Why this model is aligned

The base protects execution capacity. The 5% reward grows only when a StreamLab-sourced opportunity becomes real collected revenue. Both parties therefore benefit from qualified—not merely high-volume—lead generation.

Appendix · Definitions and safeguards

How we keep performance attribution clear.

What counts as a StreamLab-sourced opportunity?

A new opportunity whose first documented source is a StreamLab-managed event, diagnostic, tracked landing page, email nurture, founder-content CTA, outbound campaign, paid campaign, or formal referral mechanism created and tracked by StreamLab.

What counts as an accepted qualified opportunity?

A real contact within an agreed buyer profile who has a relevant organizational or individual need, has agreed to a substantive diagnostic or commercial conversation, and is accepted by the designated sales owner. Rejected leads require a documented reason in the CRM.

How are handoff delays handled?

Any delay is shown neutrally in the monthly operations scorecard so the team can distinguish source quality from process timing. The goal is not blame; it is to identify where automation, clarity, or additional support is needed.

Do all 75 implementation tactics run every month?

No. All are available within the operating system, but the monthly mix is prioritized jointly based on active events, founder inputs, funnel evidence, business urgency, and available capacity. The 236-hour estimate represents the full monthly delivery plan across the selected workstreams.

What is the review decision after 90 days?

Leadership reviews delivery completion, lead-management consistency, qualified pipeline, collected revenue, channel evidence, and the true operating workload. The parties then continue, reprioritize, resize, or conclude the engagement based on evidence.
