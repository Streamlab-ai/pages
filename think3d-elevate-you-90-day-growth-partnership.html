Appendix · Definitions and safeguards

How we keep performance attribution clear.

What counts as a StreamLab-sourced opportunity?

A new opportunity whose first documented source is a StreamLab-managed event, diagnostic, tracked landing page, email nurture, founder-content CTA, outbound campaign, paid campaign, or formal referral mechanism created and tracked by StreamLab.

What counts as an accepted qualified opportunity?

A real contact within an agreed buyer profile who has a relevant need, agrees to a substantive diagnostic or commercial conversation, and is accepted by the designated sales owner. Rejected leads require a documented reason in the CRM.

How are handoff delays handled?

Delays are shown neutrally in the monthly operations scorecard so the team can distinguish source quality from process timing. The goal is not blame; it is to identify where automation, clarity, or additional support is needed.

Do all implementation tactics run every month?

No. The monthly mix is prioritized jointly based on active events, founder inputs, funnel evidence, urgency, and available capacity.
