Brand Positioning
One-Line Identity
Transform your workplace culture. Strengthen your business.
Think3D is a strategic culture consultancy — an organizational counterpart to Elevate You's individual focus. Where Elevate You changes the person, Think3D changes the company around them.
Framework: Extract · Engage · Evaluate · Evolve
Who It Serves
Organizations & Their Leaders
Companies and leadership teams who want to build a workplace culture that boosts productivity, retention, and profitability — not individual community leaders (that's Elevate You).
The Problem
Culture Isn't an Accident
"Outstanding company cultures are not built by accident. They stem from ongoing efforts across the organization." Most companies never assess or design their culture — it just happens to them.
The Promise
A Destination for Top Talent
"We believe that we can better the world by improving people's lives by changing the way they work." Give the best professionals a compelling reason to choose you.

Culture Insight
Satisfied employees create satisfied customers.
"Outstanding company cultures are not built by accident. They stem from ongoing efforts across the organization."
  • Extract — collect essential data about your business and team
  • Engage — tailor strategies and solutions using that data
  • Evaluate — assess progress and gather feedback
  • Evolve — adjust tools, services, and coaching as needs change
    • Is your culture happening to you — or being built by you?
    #WorkplaceCulture #OrganizationalLeadership #EmployeeExperience #Think3D
    Tone of Voice
    Outcome-First
    Headlines lead with the business result, not the feeling. Culture is framed as a lever for productivity, retention, and profitability.
    "Cultivate a strong company culture that boosts productivity, enhances profitability, and drives business success."
    Systems, Not Accidents
    Culture is never framed as something that just happens — it's a system to be designed, assessed, and maintained deliberately.
    "Outstanding company cultures are not built by accident."
    Framework-Driven
    Every engagement maps to the same four-step process. Gives leadership teams something structured to follow, not just inspiration.
    Extract → Engage → Evaluate → Evolve.
    Business Case for People
    Ties employee wellbeing directly to business outcomes — talent retention, customer satisfaction, competitive advantage.
    "Satisfied employees create satisfied customers."
    Consultative, Not Hype
    Direct and measured. Speaks like a strategic partner assessing and advising — not a motivational speaker.
    "We assess progress and gather feedback to fine-tune our approach."
    Organization-Facing
    Speaks to leadership teams and companies, not individuals. The unit of change is the organization — contrast with Elevate You's individual focus.
    "Turn managers into exceptional leaders."
    Do / Don't
    Do
    • Use Heebo for all headlines and section titles
    • Use Roboto for body copy, Oxygen for nav/labels
    • Use Blue (#2ea3f2) as the primary CTA/accent color
    • Use Navy Deep (#0e2a3d) for dark backgrounds and hero sections
    • Frame every insight as a business outcome (retention, productivity, profit)
    • Map content back to Extract / Engage / Evaluate / Evolve where relevant
    • Speak to organizations and leadership teams, not individuals
    • Keep tone consultative and measured — a strategic partner, not a hype account
    Don't
    • Use green — that's Elevate You
    • Use DM Serif Display or DM Sans — that's Elevate You / Tamien's personal brand
    • Frame content through individual self-leadership — that's Elevate You
    • Use hustle culture language — no grind, crush it, go hard
    • Post inspiration without tying it back to a business outcome or the framework
    • Confuse Think3D (organizational/B2B) with Elevate You (individual/community)
    • Use gold/amber or Barlow Condensed without confirming with Ruth first — conflicts with the live site (see note above)
