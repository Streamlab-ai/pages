Grand Central
The WHY
NOVIQ's Anchor — Why People Show Up
We run your property like a business
so you don't have to.
Co-hosting is a partnership, not a handoff. Every piece of content flows from this anchor — whether it's going out to property owners, booking guests, or a new follower seeing NOVIQ for the first time. If a post doesn't connect back to this truth, it doesn't go live.
01
Destination One
Co-hosting Partnerships
Property owners in SD and LA ready to hand off — or almost ready. The primary revenue line. This is the most important destination on the map.
Who They Are
SD or LA property owner. 2+ bedroom already on Airbnb. Self-managing or working with a PM who isn't delivering.
Their Situation
Earning ~$70–100K gross/year. Burning time. Leaving money on the table. Want professional management without giving up ownership.
Where They Are
Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook groups, SD real estate investor communities. Business-minded. Respond to peer-level operator talk.
What Moves Them
Proof you know the business better than they do. Track record. Real numbers. A risk-free offer (the 6-month guarantee). Peer-level trust.
🏆
Best Signal for Destination 01
Operator Authority
This ICP is skeptical. They've been pitched by other PM companies. The thing that moves them isn't a sales message — it's seeing proof that NOVIQ understands the STR business at a depth most operators don't. When they see NOVIQ talking about dynamic pricing strategy, OTA vs. DTC tradeoffs, occupancy optimization, guest experience SOPs — they think: these people know what they're doing. I want them on my property.
Why not "brand awareness" as the primary signal: You're not trying to reach everyone — you're trying to reach the 10 property owners in SD this quarter who are ready to hand off. Authority content finds them. It also gets shared between property owners in investor groups, which is your highest-value discovery channel.

02
Destination Two
Direct Booking Guests
Past Airbnb guests who stayed at a NOVIQ property. They already have a relationship with the property. They need: (1) a reason to remember it, (2) to know they can book direct, (3) a reason to — better price or perks.
Who They Are
Past Airbnb guest at a NOVIQ property. Likely traveled to SD/LA for vacation, work, or events. Enjoyed the stay.

🔑
Best Signal for Destination 02
Property Recognition + Price Incentive
These guests already have a positive emotional connection to a NOVIQ property. The signal that carries them to a direct booking is a combination of: (1) seeing content that reminds them of that property and reignites the desire to return, and (2) a clear incentive to skip the OTA and book direct. The two work together — recognition creates desire, incentive removes friction.
Why this beats "brand awareness" for this destination: This passenger already knows NOVIQ (or the property). You don't need to introduce yourself — you need to give them a reason to act. Every content piece should include a direct path to booking or a reason the next stay should be direct.

M
The Tracking Layer
Know where every lead came from.
Without tracking, you can't tell which platform, which post type, or which piece of content is actually driving property audit requests. Three things to implement before the next post goes live.
01
UTM Parameters on Every Link
Every link you post — bio link, GMB link, booking engine — gets a UTM tag. This tells Google Analytics exactly which platform and which post drove the click. Takes 5 minutes to set up, gives you a permanent answer to "what's actually working."
02
"How did you find us?" at Every Intake
This is Grace Andrews' exact method — she always asks where someone discovered her. At every property audit inquiry, ask this question. Log it. After 10 responses you'll see a pattern. This is the ground truth that no analytics tool can fake.
03
Monthly Traffic Source Review
Once a month: look at which UTM sources sent traffic. Look at your intake log — which sources sent leads (not just clicks). The gap between clicks and leads tells you which platform has the right audience and which is just vanity traffic.
