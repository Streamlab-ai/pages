Phased Work Plan

How I would implement it

Phase 1

Brand Ecosystem Audit

  • Identify what is active, inactive, unclear, duplicated, or missing.
  • Map each brand to a simple status: optimize, launch, clarify, or defer.
  • Separate strategy problems from execution problems.
Phase 2

Funnel + Conversion Mapping

  • Document the current Monterey Bay ADU funnel as the working model.
  • Design equivalent funnels for the real estate development and AI consultancy brands.
  • Recommend the right lead magnet, CTA, and follow-up flow per priority brand.
Phase 3

AI Workflow Design

  • Create brand-specific AI instructions so each brand keeps its own voice and positioning.
  • Use AI for campaign briefs, content repurposing, post drafts, reporting summaries, and decision support.
  • Keep strategy, final approvals, paid ad decisions, and conversion analysis human-led.
Phase 4

AI Agent-Powered Marketing Operations

  • Ads management agent — monitors campaign performance, flags budget pacing issues, drafts copy variations, and surfaces optimization recommendations for review.
  • Website development and design agent — generates landing page copy, produces on-brand design briefs, and drafts page updates ready for implementation.
  • Social content engine — produces brand-specific post drafts, captions, and content calendars across all active brands on a recurring weekly cadence.
Phase 5

90-Day Launch Roadmap

  • Decide which brands get immediate attention.
  • Define what should be built, optimized, or paused.
  • Create implementation options: internal team, Ruth-led support, or StreamLab AI workflow buildout.
