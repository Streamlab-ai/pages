Make the expectation visible.

“Communicate better” can mean something different to every person on a team. Give the expectation a situation and a behavior people can recognize.

For example: “When a deadline is at risk, raise it early enough for us to discuss options.”

Work through it together. What does early enough mean here? What information is needed? How will the team respond when someone raises the issue?

Choose one expectation to make clearer this week, and set a time to review how it worked.
