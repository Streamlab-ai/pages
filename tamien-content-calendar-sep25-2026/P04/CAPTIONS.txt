What are you willing to change about the way you lead?

When we work on culture, leaders have to participate. The way you show up is part of what your people experience every day.

Choose one behavior you want to see more consistently across your team. Ask where you need to demonstrate it yourself, especially when the day gets difficult.

Teach through the clarity of your example.

From my conversation with Belinda Bradford on Full Tilt, by WEGroup Global.
