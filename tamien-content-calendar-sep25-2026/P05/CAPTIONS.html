Give one idea a place in your week.

Maybe it came from a conversation, a book or a day spent learning alongside other people. Choose the idea you can put to work in a situation you already face.

S.O.I.L. gives you a way to work with it: Soak It In. Observe. Implement. Look Back.

Notice where the idea applies. Practice one behavior. Then return to what happened and learn from it.

Save this and choose the situation where you’ll begin.
