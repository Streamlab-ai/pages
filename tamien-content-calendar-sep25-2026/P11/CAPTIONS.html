What changed after you tried it?

If you chose a practice earlier this month, return to a real moment when you used it. What did you notice? What helped? Where did it become difficult?

This is the Look Back part of S.O.I.L. Give yourself something more useful than a general feeling that you did well or fell short.

Choose what you’ll keep, what you’ll adjust and when you’ll try again.

If you haven’t started yet, choose one situation this week. Progress gives you something to learn from.
