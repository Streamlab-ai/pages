Tamien’s LinkedIn

Thank you for making Elevating CommYOUnity: The Ripple Experience a success.

To everyone who showed up, joined the conversations and brought their energy into the room: thank you. Over 140 people came together to invest in themselves and in our community, and I’m grateful to have shared that day with you.

Together with our sponsors and attendees, we raised $2,000 for The Grateful Homie Project, a peer-led recovery clubhouse.

A special thank-you to First PREMIER Bank, Vern Eide, VOA Dakotas, First Bank & Trust, Sanford Health and Premier Business Services, Inc. for helping make it possible.

These moments capture the people and connections that made the day special. Thank you for being part of it.
