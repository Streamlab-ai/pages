What happened after you asked?

When we invite people to share what could be better, the next conversation matters.

Return to what you heard. Explain what you can act on, what needs more work and what you cannot change right now.

Give people a clear next step and a time when you will come back to it.

That follow-through gives your team something concrete to work with.
