What version of you comes home?

In my conversation with Belinda Bradford on Full Tilt, I shared a moment in my driveway that made me question what my work was costing the people I loved.

That reflection was part of a longer transition. It asked me to get honest about the life I was building and the way I was showing up in it.

Before you set your next professional goal, make room for this question: what do I want the people closest to me to experience from me?
