Your team watches what happens next.

When someone brings you a missed deadline, a request for help or a concern, your response becomes part of the culture they experience.

Do you make room to understand the situation? Do you clarify what matters next? Do you follow through on the support you offered?

Those everyday moments give your values a visible form.

Choose one situation this week. Ask your team what would make your response more useful, then practice it.
