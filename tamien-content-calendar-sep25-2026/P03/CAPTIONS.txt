Listening is only the beginning.

At a recent community listening session, we worked through a question I keep coming back to: what can we do together?

There is a leadership practice inside that question. Before bringing your team another answer, give them room to describe what they are experiencing.

Try asking: “What is making it harder for us to do good work?” Listen without defending. Then agree on one next action, who will own it and when you will return to the conversation.

Make it clear that what people share has somewhere to go.
