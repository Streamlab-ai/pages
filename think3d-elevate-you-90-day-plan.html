How to Read This Plan

Days 1–30

Define the category and conversion foundation.

Before a single new campaign goes out, both businesses need their own category language, their own diagnostic, and their own conversion path. Most of week one is decisions we already have the context to make from running both businesses' systems today — the few genuine open items go straight to the kickoff, not a round of approvals.

Open items for the kickoff — not a checklist, an agenda

  • Named category and signature methodology for each business — this is Tamien, Vaney, and Matt's actual IP, so it's theirs to set, not ours to assume
  • Final scope of each proprietary diagnostic, matched against the real programs and frameworks already in use, not a generic template
  • Finalize Think3D and Elevate You commercial owner assignments and lead-routing rules based on the current team structure
  • Lock CRM ownership boundaries — HubSpot for Think3D, Pipedrive for Elevate You — and finalize measurement definitions
  • Scope the shape of each proprietary diagnostic based on what we already know of each program, ready to align live at kickoff
  • Draft the Think3D culture/leadership diagnostic (Culture Partners / Table Group pattern)
  • Draft the Elevate You self-leadership / behavior-under-pressure diagnostic (BetterUp / Goldsmith pattern)
  • Complete Tamien and Vaney LinkedIn conversion audits and featured-section updates
  • Translate the already-confirmed Elevate You five-tier product menu into buyer-facing language, with the Pilot → Embed → Scale explanation
  • Build the proof-capture checklist template used for every future event, workshop, cohort, and engagement
  • Approve final diagnostic builds and the executive-debrief format for each business
  • Build the primary diagnostic/executive-briefing lead magnet for Think3D and a separate one for Elevate You
  • Establish the monthly founder source-content recording cadence — first sessions scheduled
  • Build the September 22 landing page, Zeffy checkout, and campaign plan against the confirmed brief
  • Carry Kansas City and Jacksonville nurture workflows through to completion
  • Build the integrated monthly marketing and pipeline scorecard
  • Prepare separate regional Apollo and nurture systems for Think3D and Elevate You

  • Launch one recurring executive webinar per business — Think3D on culture/leadership topics (Culture Partners / Arbinger cadence), Elevate You on self-leadership/behavior-under-pressure topics (Wiseman Group cadence)

Phase 2 deliverable

Consistent business-specific authority content, two recurring executive education formats, owned-audience capture, diagnostic conversion paths, and event/nurture workflows fully operational.

Days 61–90

Convert, prove, and optimize.

The last phase turns two months of activity into evidence — and starts cutting whatever isn't converting.

  • Review content and campaign performance from weeks 1–8; identify what's actually converting
  • Publish problem-specific case studies for Think3D, framed in business-performance language — revenue, execution, retention — the way Culture Partners does
  • Publish the first behavioral-to-business-outcome case material for Elevate You — the same outcome-led framing BetterUp uses, without overstating unverified results
  • Deploy buyer-enablement guides and implementation FAQs for both businesses
  • Promote proven assets; launch selective ABM, regional outbound, or paid tests only where organic proof and tracking are already working
  • Tighten CRM follow-up — enforced named owner, next-activity, SLA reminders, and stalled-lead review
  • Begin partner and referral distribution outreach — chambers, associations, HR networks — for both businesses
  • Full 90-day review against the operational targets below
  • Present what continues into the standing monthly cadence — this doesn't end at day 90, it becomes how the retainer runs
Phase 3 deliverable

Qualified pipeline reporting, a documented proof library, buyer-enablement assets, optimized content themes, and a repeatable, business-specific campaign model for both Think3D and Elevate You.

Built On What Already Works

What we're adapting from the category leaders.

Every build item above traces back to a specific, proven pattern from the benchmark. Here's the direct line from "what they do" to "what we're building," by business.

Think3D

  • Own a named category — not generic "culture consulting." Culture Partners' "Change Activation" model is the template.
  • Client logos and business-outcome case studies — the single highest-leverage public demand signal a category leader can show. This is the one place we need Think3D's help directly: client logos and permission to use them across the diagnostic, the website, and future collateral, framed in revenue/retention/execution language, the way Culture Partners does.
  • Branded diagnostic + executive debrief as the serious, low-risk entry point.
  • Recurring executive webinars connecting culture to accountability, execution, and leadership-team dysfunction (Culture Partners / Arbinger cadence).
  • Downloadable practical tools — check-ins, conversation guides — from Tamien and Vaney's frameworks (Arbinger pattern).
  • One flagship executive-team entry engagement with a clear duration and output (The Table Group pattern).

Elevate You

  • Explain how the behavioral system connects to business outcomes — productivity, retention, performance — without overstating unverified results. This is BetterUp's core move, and it's the single most important piece of messaging we build for Elevate You this quarter.
  • Stakeholder-evidence loop — behavior change validated by the people around the leader, not just self-reported (Marshall Goldsmith pattern).
  • Productized self-leadership entry point with clear delivery formats — public experience, pilot, cohort (Blanchard pattern).
  • Self-leadership diagnostic + newsletter + monthly live session as the owned-audience engine (Wiseman Group pattern).
  • Enterprise-style proof, privacy, and security messaging for organizational buyers evaluating a pilot (BetterUp pattern).
